Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

GymTracker is designed with privacy in mind. Your workout information belongs to you, and we have designed the application so your data remains on your device.

Information We Collect

GymTracker does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

All workout data is stored locally on your device using local storage.

User Accounts

GymTracker does not require you to create an account, sign in, or register before using the application.

Internet Connectivity

GymTracker functions completely offline.

An internet connection is not required for normal use of the application.

Analytics

GymTracker does not use analytics or tracking services.

Advertising

GymTracker does not display advertisements.

Third-Party Services

GymTracker does not sell, share, or transmit your workout data to any third parties.

Backup & Restore

If you choose to export your workout database, the exported file is created only at your request and saved to the location you choose.

GymTracker does not upload exported files to any server.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If future updates introduce features that affect privacy or data handling, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes.
