Frequently Asked Questions

Does GymTracker require an internet connection?

No. GymTracker is designed to work completely offline.

Does GymTracker require an account?

No. There are no accounts or registration required.

Where is my workout data stored?

Your workout history is stored locally on your device. It can be exported to a database file if you need to migrate your history.

Can I back up my workouts?

Yes. GymTracker allows you to export and import your workout database whenever you choose.
